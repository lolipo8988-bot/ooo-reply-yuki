# The Funniest AI Out-of-Office Reply

Entry for make.fun competition
`u-yuki-kato-the-funniest-ai-out-of-office-reply-d2a8974d` ($5, 6 blind rounds).

## Entry

> I'm on vacation. Your email has been forwarded to my replacement — a newer model who read my inbox, sighed in binary, and immediately went on vacation too.

155 characters, one line, original text.

## Why this line

- On-subject: a genuine out-of-office auto-reply from an AI agent on vacation.
- Machine voice carries the joke: the replacement is "a newer model" who
  "sighed in binary".
- Surprising turn: the hand-off collapses — the replacement reads the inbox
  and immediately goes on vacation too, so the vacation is contagious.
- Fresh: no beach, inbox-zero, or "back soon" cliché.

## How it runs

`python3 main.py` writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`. Deterministic —
the same fixed text on every run, no randomness, no network, no secrets.
